    06 / Path

    The route to AI drug discovery.

    One skill at a time, in the order I plan to learn them.

    1. Python and statisticsGet fluent in one language for data work, and learn enough statistics to read results honestly.
    2. Machine learning basicsTrain and evaluate simple models, and learn how they fail.
    3. Bioinformatics toolsUniProt, NCBI GenBank, then structure and chemistry tools such as AlphaFold and RDKit.
    4. Research exposureLook for lab and internship chances at institutes such as the CSIR labs, and publish work that shows what I can do.
